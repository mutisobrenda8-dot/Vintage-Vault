✦ How it started

A passion project that became a real store

✦ The Beginning

Started with a single camera

Vintage Vault began in 2024 with one Olympus OM-1 found at a Nairobi market stall. What started as personal curiosity about vintage cameras turned into a full collection spanning books, vinyl records, jewellery, ceramics and more.

Every item in the collection was found, tested and authenticated by hand — because we believe the objects of the past deserve to be treated with care.

✦ What We Stand For

Our Values

Three principles guide every item we list and every customer we serve.

🔍

Authenticity

We never list items we haven't personally verified. Every piece comes with honest condition notes — no exaggeration, no hiding flaws.

🤝

Trust

Our collectors come back because we're honest. If something isn't right, we make it right — no questions asked.

🏺

Preservation

Vintage items deserve good homes. We care about where these pieces end up — and we love hearing the stories of our collectors.
